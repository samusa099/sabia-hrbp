# 🧭 Executive Mandate

## Primary mandate

> **Determine whether the current workforce situation represents an isolated people issue or a broader failure involving workforce planning, management control, policy governance, employee experience and organisational oversight.**

## Supporting questions

1. Which management statements are supported by current data?
2. Which statements are plausible but unverified?
3. Which claims cannot be assessed from available evidence?
4. Has workforce growth created stability, or masked replacement dependency?
5. Is risk materially concentrated by department, location, employment type, tenure or demographic segment?
6. Are HR policies, approval practices and decision rights sufficiently clear and consistently governed?
7. Which attendance, leave, promotion, performance and grievance records are missing?
8. Which current Bangladesh legal or regulatory issues are relevant?
9. Which actions are safe to implement now?
10. Which actions require additional investigation?
11. Which metrics should management review after 90 and 180 days?
12. How should the HR data architecture be improved to prevent repeated uncertainty?

## Required decision structure

Your management position must separate:

- **Immediate controls** — low-regret actions supported by current evidence;
- **Investigative actions** — evidence collection, interviews, audits or validation;
- **Conditional interventions** — proceed only if defined evidence thresholds are met;
- **Longer-term changes** — systems, capability, policy and governance improvements;
- **Do-not-act items** — decisions that would be unsafe or indefensible now.
