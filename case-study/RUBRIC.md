# 🧾 Assessment Rubric

| Criterion | Weight | Strong evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Evidence discipline | 20 | Clearly separates verified evidence, management accounts, allegations, external research and gaps |
| Problem framing | 15 | Defines the material management problem without assuming a predetermined answer |
| Analytical validity | 15 | Reconciles metrics, segments appropriately and states limitations |
| Role-based decision quality | 15 | Recommendation matches selected authority and professional responsibilities |
| Policy and legal research | 15 | Uses current official sources and connects them to controls and missing evidence |
| Recommendations | 10 | Separates immediate, investigative, conditional and long-term actions |
| 90/180-day measurement | 5 | Defines owners, metrics, evidence and review cadence |
| Communication and reproducibility | 5 | Clear report, references, tools and reproducible analysis |

## Automatic ineligibility

A submission may be marked not eligible when it:

- includes real employee or confidential data;
- modifies official project files;
- embeds duplicate datasets;
- contains credentials or secrets;
- presents allegations as facts;
- fabricates legal sources;
- exceeds file or path restrictions;
- omits `README.md` or `report.pdf`.

## Review outcomes

- `accepted`
- `revision-required`
- `not-eligible`

A merged submission is not guaranteed. Feedback may remain in pull-request history.
