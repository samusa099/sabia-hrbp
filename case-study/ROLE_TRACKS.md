# 🎭 Role Tracks

Select one primary role. A hybrid track may combine two roles, but the primary decision-maker must be explicit.

| Track | Decision authority | Main focus | Required output emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Managing Director / Executive | Approves priorities, controls and investment | Enterprise risk, accountability, intervention approval | Board-ready position and decision gates |
| HR Business Partner | Designs people interventions and advises management | Department diagnosis, retention risk, ER, management practice | Evidence-based intervention portfolio |
| People Analytics | Validates and interprets workforce evidence | Data quality, metrics, segmentation, trends and limitations | Reproducible analysis and evidence map |
| Operations and Finance | Prioritises continuity and resources | Capacity, replacement pressure, cost, recruitment efficiency | Scenario model and resource recommendation |
| Policy and Governance | Defines controls and auditability | Policy consistency, authority, legal alignment, HR data governance | Control framework and evidence requirements |

## Hybrid examples

- HRBP + People Analytics
- Managing Director + Policy Governance
- Operations + Workforce Planning

## Universal obligations

Every role must:

- distinguish evidence from opinion and allegation;
- document limitations;
- avoid causal claims not supported by evidence;
- include policy and legal research;
- propose a 90/180-day measurement framework;
- avoid adding real employee data.
