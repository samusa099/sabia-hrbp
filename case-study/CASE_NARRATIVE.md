# 📖 Case Narrative

Sabia Group expanded its smartwatch manufacturing operations during 2026. Recruitment activity increased and the workforce grew, but several units continued to miss targets. Replacement requisitions became routine, onboarding work accumulated, experienced-employee continuity weakened and line managers repeatedly reported manpower pressure.

Finance questioned whether repeated hiring was solving a capacity problem or financing instability. Operations raised concerns about discipline, attendance and production continuity. Department heads blamed market scarcity and competitor hiring. Employees described inconsistent flexibility, supervisor conduct and limited career visibility, but the complaints were neither complete nor consistently documented. HR acknowledged that records were fragmented and that stakeholder explanations conflicted.

The Managing Director requested a defensible management position. No intervention would be approved merely because one stakeholder’s explanation sounded plausible.

## What changed

After operational complaints had accumulated, HR reviewed the existing project evidence. Patterns became visible, but the evidence did not automatically establish causation or identify a single root cause. Management then expanded the review beyond workforce stability to include:

- policy consistency;
- attendance and leave governance;
- promotion and career controls;
- managerial accountability;
- contract management;
- employee relations and grievance evidence;
- HR data governance;
- current Bangladesh labour-law and regulatory alignment.

## Your task

You are not asked to produce a predetermined answer. You must determine whether the situation is:

- an isolated workforce issue;
- an operational-capacity problem;
- a management-control problem;
- a policy-governance problem;
- a people-data problem;
- or a material combination of these.

Your final position must distinguish what is verified, what is plausible but unverified, what cannot be assessed, what requires external research and what action is safe to take now.
