# 🧩 Management Context

The following statements are **management accounts**, not established facts.

| Stakeholder | Statement | Evidence status at case start |
|---|---|---|
| Operations | Attendance and discipline are disrupting continuity. | Plausible; requires attendance, shift and supervisor evidence |
| Finance | Repeated replacement hiring is increasing cost without stabilising output. | Partly testable through hiring, cost, workforce and production data |
| Department heads | Competitor hiring and skill scarcity explain most exits. | Plausible; external-market and exit evidence incomplete |
| Employees | Flexibility, supervisor conduct and career stagnation are inconsistent. | Allegation / partial account; grievance and promotion records required |
| HR | Records are fragmented and explanations are mutually inconsistent. | Testable through data-quality and governance review |
| Managing Director | A rapid decision is needed, but unsupported intervention will not be approved. | Executive constraint |

## Required evidence classification

For each material conclusion, state:

1. the claim;
2. its evidence category;
3. the supporting source;
4. the limitation;
5. the next decision or evidence requirement.

## Prohibited reasoning

- Treating allegations as verified facts
- Inferring misconduct from turnover alone
- Claiming legal non-compliance without current official evidence
- Claiming causation from correlation
- Presenting modelled savings as realised financial benefit
- Recommending adverse employment action without individual due process and supporting records
