# ⚖️ Policy, Governance and Legal Research

This section is mandatory. Legal and regulatory facts must be verified using **current official sources at the submission date**.

| Required item | Expectation |
|---|---|
| Applicable issue | Identify the legal, policy or regulatory topic |
| Official source | Government, gazette, ministry or recognised official publication |
| Relevance | Explain the connection to the case concern |
| Dataset relationship | State whether current data can assess the issue |
| Evidence limitation | Identify missing records or facts |
| Recommended control | Propose policy, approval, documentation or reporting changes |

## Research rules

- Do not rely only on blogs, social posts or unsourced summaries.
- Do not quote a legal provision without explaining its relevance.
- Distinguish law, rule, policy, internal practice and professional recommendation.
- Do not declare non-compliance when the required evidence is absent.
- Record source title, issuing authority, publication date, access date and link.

## Suggested governance topics

Attendance and leave, working time, contract documentation, disciplinary process, termination, grievance handling, promotion criteria, equal treatment, records retention, worker participation, privacy and HR data governance.

The case intentionally does not embed an unverified claim about a “new labour law.” Current requirements must be researched independently.
